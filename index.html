How to use the atlas

Move from the broad regulatory mechanism to the specific evidence you want to examine. Your selected route stays visible across the top of the map.

  1. Choose a family and subtype.

    Families group related regulatory approaches. Select a subtype to trace one mechanism; use the ? on a selected family or subtype for its definition.

  2. Choose what to examine.

    Potential effects comes first. You can then switch to the relevant laws and proposals or compare current practices at Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind.

  3. Trace effects through the firm.

    Choose Inputs or Development & deployment pipeline, then select a specific input or stage. Muted points remain visible when no significant impact is identified there.

  4. Read the evidence at the endpoint.

    The panel beside the selected branch shows the effect, legal requirement, or closest public lab analogue.

Share a route: every selection updates the page address. Copy the URL to send someone directly to the same branch.

About this guide

Abstract

The Anatomy of Compliance

This paper examines how AI regulation affects frontier model developers. It reviews 37 enacted and proposed policy instruments, organizing them into six regulatory families and tracing their effects across key inputs and stages of model development.

AI regulation costs more than just paperwork. This guide shows where each rule could slow development, consume scarce resources, or change products. It also compares each rule with current lab practices, showing how much labs may need to change to comply.

Direct compliance expenses—such as compute for evaluations and runtime controls—are often modest. Larger costs arise from release delays, product degradation, security requirements, and legal exposure. Because frontier developers already follow many proposed practices voluntarily, regulation will often formalize existing activities. External verification, prescribed standards, and liability can nevertheless change marginal incentives and advantage developers able to spread fixed costs across a larger revenue base.
